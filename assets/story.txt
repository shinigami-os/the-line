# The welcome story: every text of it. Edit freely, nothing else needs to change.
#
# `beat.N = text`: the paragraphs of a beat, shown one after the other (N = 1, 2, 3...). The first one is the big one.
# *words* are lit in the accent. {user} {host} {uptime} {packages}... are filled in live, like Kira's quotes.
# Lines starting with # are comments.

hello.1 = welcome to *The Line*
hello.2 = a shell based only on one Line, to keep things simple.
hello.3 = *→* to go on, *←* to go back, *esc* to leave. i will not be offended don't worry (well, maybe a little)
hello.offer = if animations make you queasy, say so now. i'll tell the story standing still.

line.1 = this is *the line*.
line.2 = everything lives on it. no dock, no taskbar, no panel pretending to be a desktop.
line.3 = that little bar is the *accent*. it has opinions about colour. so, presumably, do you.
line.offer = pick one, or let the wallpaper decide. it has good taste. (it hasn't shown up yet. patience.)

workspaces.1 = on the left, your *workspaces*. ten of them, hanging from the line like laundry. elegant laundry.
workspaces.2 = *super + a number* goes there. *super + scroll* goes there but faster.
workspaces.3 = under them, the name of what you're doing. or "desktop", when you're just admiring our wallpaper (no judgement)

launcher.1 = tap *super*, and the tile grows into a *launcher*.
launcher.2 = it finds apps, solves sums, runs commands, and knows where the settings are. type *?* and it recites its prefixes, like a waiter reciting the menu.
launcher.3 = i typed "fir" for you. you're welcome.

clock.1 = in the middle, the hour, and the *notifications*. they arrive like this.
clock.2 = *super + n* opens them, with a calendar. there is a do-not-disturb, for days when the world can wait.
clock.notify.title = test de Juilen à nouveau
clock.notify.body = (did you get it ?)

status.1 = on the right, the state of the machine: processor, memory, wi-fi, volume, battery.
status.2 = *super + a* opens the quick settings. *super + p* tells you everything the machine is doing, in more detail than you asked for.

levels.1 = volume and brightness don't pop up in a box. the line *becomes* the bar. look.
levels.2 = no box. no dialog. just the line, doing its job.

voice.1 = when a workspace is empty, i talk. quietly. at the bottom left.
voice.2 = i'm told some people prefer silence. i'd rather not know which.
voice.line = {user}@{host}. we've met.
voice.offer = so: do you keep me, or do i go?

fullscreen.1 = when a window goes *fullscreen*, the tiles fold into the line, and the line steps aside. what a gentleman.
fullscreen.2 = push the pointer against the top edge for a moment and it comes back. then leaves again, discreetly.

keys.1 = you will forget things. it's normal. keep one: *super + f1*. every shortcut, on one sheet.
keys.2 = and, in passing: *super + i* settings · *super + l* lock · *super + t* wallpapers · *super + v* clipboard.
keys.3 = PS : you should try *super+m* while playing some music ^^

eggs.1 = there are *easter eggs*. i won't say where. i won't say how many.
eggs.2 = finding them is your job. i'm only the narrator.

end.1 = the useful bit. no telemetry: what happens here stays here.
end.2 = your settings live in *~/.config/the-line/*, with a readme that says who writes what. the settings app is *super + i*.
end.3 = to hear all this again, type *welcome* in the launcher. i'll pretend it's the first time.
end.4 = that's all, {user}. the line is yours.
